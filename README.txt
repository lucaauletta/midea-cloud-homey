Midea is the world's largest manufacturer of air treatment products, and its air conditioners are sold as Midea, Comfee, Carrier, Senville, Inventor, Artel and other brands that use the NetHome Plus app. This app brings those units into Homey: switch them on and off, pick the mode, set the temperature, adjust fan speed and swing, enable Turbo, ECO or freeze protection, and use every function in your Flows.

It works entirely through the Midea cloud, the same way the NetHome Plus app does when you are away from home, so pairing keeps working now that Midea has closed the service that local integrations relied on. All you need is your NetHome Plus account and an internet connection.
